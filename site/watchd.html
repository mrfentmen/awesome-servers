watchd

The same keyless feeds, polled all day, with a push when a threshold is met.

registrypublic-feed atlas

The packages in this repo answer when asked. The flood stage does not care whether anyone asked. watchd closes that gap: it polls the same feeds the agents were verified against, evaluates plain thresholds and notifies whatever channel you hand it — console, JSONL file, or a webhook.

cd watchd
cp conditions.example.json conditions.json   # edit places, thresholds, notifiers
python3 watchd.py --config conditions.json --once     # one pass (cron-friendly)
python3 watchd.py --config conditions.json --loop     # poll forever

What it watches, keyless

kindsourcequestion
airOpen-Meteo air quality (CAMS)"is the US AQI over my threshold right now?"
quakesUSGS event service"did a quake of M X land within Y km in the look-back?"
floodNWS NWPS gauges"is the gauge at or past its published flood stage?"

Every fired alert carries the reading it saw — "US AQI 162 (over your threshold 150)", "M4.9 — 12 km from Fresno, 3 min ago" — so a notification never asks you to trust a bare flag. A feed that did not answer is logged as a feed problem; it is never an alert.

The honest version

Watcher-grade push is a margin-of-safety question. watchd tells you a threshold was met with the reading that met it; it does not replace gauge telemetry, official alerting or emergency instructions. Conditions zones are checked on a timer, so a reading that arrives and leaves between polls can be missed — the flood and quakes checks read each feed's own look-back window exactly to cover that gap.
