# Whiskyhunter MCP

## Tagline
Whisky auction data from WhiskyHunter. No key required.

## Description
Whisky auction data from WhiskyHunter. No key required. Published from a private commercial monorepo. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Whiskyhunter.

## Setup Requirements
- No configuration required. Works out of the box with no API keys.

## Category
Ecommerce

## Features

- Auctions: Whisky auction results: mean winning bid, trading volume and lots sold per month. Optionally filter to one auction house by slug, e.g. 'bonhams'.
- Auctions by date: Every auction house's results for one month, ranked by trading volume.
- Auction summary: Aggregate stats for one auction house, or the whole whisky auction market: bids, volumes and lots.
- Top auctions: Rank whisky auction houses by total trading volume, lots sold, or average winning bid.
- Market trend: Mean winning bid per month over time, for one auction house or the whole market.
- Auction seasonality: Show which calendar months historically fetch the highest mean winning bid and volume, averaged across every year in the dataset.
- Compare auctions: Compare two or more auction houses side by side: record counts, date range, mean and highest winning bid, total volume and their latest months.
- Market momentum: Compare the most recent months of whisky auction results against the months before them: mean winning bid, trading volume and lots, with the percent change.
- Monthly leaderboard: Rank auction houses over the last few recorded months by trading volume, lots sold or mean winning bid, including each house's share of the volume traded.
- Head to head: Two auction houses month by month on volume, lots or winning bid: which one won each month, the tally, and the all-time totals.
