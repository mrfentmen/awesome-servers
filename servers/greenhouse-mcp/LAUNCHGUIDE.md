# Greenhouse MCP

## Tagline
Jobs from Greenhouse boards. No key required.

## Description
Jobs from Greenhouse boards. No key required. Published from a private commercial monorepo. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Greenhouse.

## Setup Requirements
- No configuration required. Works out of the box with no API keys.

## Category
Jobs

## Features

- Jobs: List open jobs on a company Greenhouse board, with location, departments and job ids.
- Job detail: Get one job posting on a Greenhouse board, including the full description text.
- Search jobs: Search a company Greenhouse board by title, location, department or office.
- Departments: List the teams hiring on a Greenhouse board, ranked by number of open roles.
- Offices: List the office locations on a Greenhouse board.
- Board overview: Show a company Greenhouse board: name, open job count and the company intro.
- Jobs by office: List the open jobs attached to one office on a Greenhouse board, or to a matching location.
- Newest jobs: List the most recently opened jobs on a Greenhouse board, newest first.
- Application form: Show the real application questions for one Greenhouse job, with which are required and the deadline.
