# Smartrecruiters MCP

## Tagline
Jobs from SmartRecruiters. No key required.

## Description
Jobs from SmartRecruiters. No key required. Published from a private commercial monorepo. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Smartrecruiters.

## Setup Requirements
- No configuration required. Works out of the box with no API keys.

## Category
Jobs

## Features

- Jobs: List a company SmartRecruiters job postings with location, department and posting id.
- Posting detail: Get one SmartRecruiters posting by id, with the full job description and apply link.
- Search postings: Search a company SmartRecruiters board by title, location, department or function.
- Hiring breakdown: Show a company SmartRecruiters hiring split by department, function, industry and experience level.
- Search all companies: Search SmartRecruiters job postings across every company at once, with optional country and remote filters.
- Companies hiring: Rank the companies posting the most SmartRecruiters jobs for a keyword, with a sample role each.
- Page a board: Page deeper into a company board by offset, so a board with thousands of postings can be read past the newest hundred.
- Postings by country: One country's postings from a company board, filtered by the API itself, with the share of the whole board that country represents.
- Board overview: A company board as a whole: posting count, countries, departments, experience levels, contract types and how much of it is remote.
