# Gold Prices MCP

## Tagline
Gold and metal prices from gold-api.com (merged).

## Description
Gold and metal prices from gold-api.com (merged). Published from a private commercial monorepo. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Gold Prices.

## Setup Requirements
- No configuration required. Works out of the box with no API keys.

## Category
Finance

## Features

- Price: Live spot price for one metal or asset in USD, with the quote source and timestamp. Accepts a ticker (XAU) or a name (gold).
- All precious metals: Spot prices for the four precious metals in one call: gold, silver, platinum and palladium.
- Market overview: One call for every asset the price feed tracks — precious metals, copper, Bitcoin and Ethereum — with live USD prices.
- Symbols: The ticker symbols this price feed supports, so an agent knows which values price and convert accept.
- Convert: Value an amount of one asset in USD, or in another tracked asset, using live prices. Example: 2 ounces of gold in USD, or 100 grams of silver in gold.
- Portfolio value: Value a list of metal and crypto holdings in USD at live prices, with each holding's share of the total. Example: XAU:2, XAG:100, BTC:0.5.
- Asset ratios: Live ratios traders watch — gold/silver, gold/platinum, gold/palladium, gold/copper and bitcoin/gold — both sides quoted at once.
