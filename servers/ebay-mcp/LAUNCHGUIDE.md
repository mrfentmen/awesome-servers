## Category
Business Tools

## Features
- get_item_group: Multi-seller listing group.
- get_legacy_item: Lookup by legacy ID.
- deal_items: eBay Top Deals.
- search_extended: Full details search
- search_ending_soon: Auctions ending first.
- search_lowest_price: Cheapest first.
- search_newly_listed: Newest first.
- search_by_category: Scoped search.

- Search items: eBay listings matching a query, with price and condition.
- Get item: One listing by item id, with price and seller.

## Getting Started

- "Find this on eBay"
- "Price check?"
- Tool: search_items — eBay listings matching a query, with price and condition.
- Tool: get_item — One listing by item id, with price and seller.
